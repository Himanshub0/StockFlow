# User Personas: StockFlow

## Primary Persona: Warehouse Operations Specialist
- **Name:** Marcus Chen
- **Role:** Floor Inventory Manager
- **Goals:** Rapid SKU scanning, logging inbound pallet receipts without delays, immediate visibility of out-of-stock items.
- **Pain Points:** Complex multi-step ERP checkout dialogs, slow desktop software, latency during stock audits.
- **Key Actions:** Fast search (Cmd+K), quick stock intake (INBOUND), instant barcode/SKU reconciliation.

## Secondary Persona: Logistics Lead & Purchasing
- **Name:** Elena Vance
- **Role:** Supply Chain Director
- **Goals:** Track supplier performance metrics, manage lead times, monitor replenishment safety buffers.
- **Pain Points:** Unexpected stockouts, lack of audit trails on inventory shrinkage.
- **Key Actions:** Supplier directory tracking, review low-stock thresholds, export transaction history.
