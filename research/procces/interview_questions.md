# User Research Interview Questions: StockFlow
> Questions used during semi-structured field interviews with small business owners and shop staff in Ahmedabad, Surat, and Vadodara (Gujarat). Interviews conducted in Gujarati and Hindi; responses later translated and coded.

---

## Section A — Current Stock Management Practices

1. Walk me through what you do from the moment a supplier delivery arrives at your shop until that stock shows up as "available" in your system. How many steps does it take?

2. How do you currently track stock — notebook, Excel, billing software, POS, or something else? If you use more than one, how do you keep them in sync?

3. How do you find out that a product is running low or has finished? Do you get a warning in advance, or do you usually discover it only when a customer asks for it or the shelf is empty?

4. How long does it take to find the current stock level of a specific item when a customer asks on the phone? Walk me through what you actually do in that moment.

5. When you receive a delivery, how do you verify that the quantities match what you ordered and what the supplier's challan says?

---

## Section B — Pain Points and Failures

6. Tell me about a time when a stockout caused you a real problem — lost sale, unhappy customer, or a supplier dispute. What happened and what was the root cause?

7. What is the biggest source of mismatch between your records and your physical stock? (Wrong entries, theft, damaged goods, unrecorded sales, supplier short-deliveries?)

8. How often do you have to do a full physical stock count (*stock taka*)? How long does it take, and how accurate are the results?

9. Have you ever ordered too much of something because records showed less than actual stock? Or ordered too little because records showed more? What happened?

10. If you could remove one painful, repetitive task from your daily routine related to stock management, what would it be?

---

## Section C — Supplier and Purchasing Workflow

11. How many suppliers do you work with regularly? How do you decide which supplier to order from for a given item — price, reliability, lead time?

12. How do you track whether a supplier delivered on time and in full? Do you have any record of their past performance?

13. When you decide to place an order, how do you determine the quantity? Memory, past sales data, gut feeling, or something else?

14. What happens when a supplier is delayed — how do you find out, and how does it affect your stock situation?

---

## Section D — Technology and Device Preferences

15. Which devices do you use in the shop — desktop computer, laptop, tablet, mobile? Which one do you use most often for stock-related tasks?

16. Have you tried any inventory or billing software before? What worked well and what made you stop using it (or never start)?

17. How comfortable are you learning a new app or software tool? Would you prefer to be trained by someone, or do you prefer to explore on your own?

18. What matters more to you in a stock management tool — having many features, or having a simple system that does the basics perfectly?

19. Would you use the app more from your phone or from a desktop computer? What tasks would you want to do on each?

---

## Section E — Willingness to Adopt

20. If a system could send you a WhatsApp or in-app alert the moment any item drops below your minimum stock level, how much would that change your daily routine?

21. What would make you trust a digital stock count over your physical notebook or memory?

22. If this app were available today, what is the first thing you would want to do with it?
