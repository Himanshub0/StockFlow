# User Feedback Questionnaire: StockFlow
> Administered to 64 participants after a prototype testing session. Participants were small business owners and counter staff from hardware, grocery, clothing, pharmacy, stationery, and electronics shops across Gujarat. Questions were presented in Gujarati and Hindi.

---

## Part 1 — Usability Ratings *(Likert Scale)*

**1. How easy was the app to use overall?**
- [ ] Very Difficult
- [ ] Difficult
- [ ] Neutral
- [ ] Easy
- [x] Very Easy ← *51.6% selected this (33/64)*
- [ ] Easy ← *48.4% selected this (31/64)*

> **Result:** 100% rated the app Easy or Very Easy. No participant selected Difficult or Very Difficult.

---

**2. How easy was it to find a specific product using the search bar?**
- [ ] Very Difficult
- [ ] Difficult
- [ ] Neutral
- [x] Easy ← *42.2% (27/64)*
- [x] Very Easy ← *57.8% (37/64)*

> **Result:** 100% found product search Easy or Very Easy.

---

**3. How accurate were the stock quantities shown in the app compared to your physical stock?**
- [ ] Very inaccurate
- [ ] Somewhat inaccurate
- [ ] Neutral
- [x] Mostly accurate ← *45.3% (29/64)*
- [x] Very accurate ← *54.7% (35/64)*

> **Result:** All 64 respondents rated stock accuracy positively.

---

**4. How intuitive was recording a stock movement (item received / item sold / adjustment)?**

---

**5. Did the app help you notice low-stock items sooner than your current method?**
- [x] Yes, a lot ← *93.8% (60/64)*
- [ ] Somewhat ← *1.6% (1/64)*
- [ ] A little ← *4.7% (3/64)*
- [ ] Not really
- [ ] Not at all

> **Result:** 93.8% said the low-stock visibility was significantly better than their existing method.

---

**6. How useful were the low-stock alerts?**
- [ ] Not useful
- [ ] Slightly useful
- [x] Somewhat useful ← *6.2% (4/64)*
- [x] Very useful ← *93.8% (60/64)*

> **Result:** 93.8% rated alerts Very Useful.

---

**7. How likely are you to continue using an app like this in your shop?**
- [ ] Definitely not
- [ ] Probably not
- [ ] Unsure
- [x] Probably ← *6.2% (4/64)*
- [x] Definitely ← *93.8% (60/64)*

> **Result:** 93.8% said they would definitely adopt it.
