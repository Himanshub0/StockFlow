# User Research Interview Questions

1. Walk me through your typical daily routine when receiving inbound warehouse stock.
2. How do you currently track items when stock falls below safety levels?
3. What is the biggest source of inventory discrepancy in your day-to-day work?
4. When performing an inter-facility or inter-bin transfer, what information must be captured?
5. How often do you audit supplier performance ratings and lead times?
6. Which device do you primarily use when checking stock on the warehouse floor (desktop, tablet, handheld scanner)?
