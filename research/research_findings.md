# UX Research Findings Summary

## Key Insights

1. **Search Speed is Critical:** Operators prioritize immediate SKU filtering with keyboard shortcuts (`Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K`) over complex multi-field filters.
2. **Clear Feedback on Insufficient Stock:** Users strongly prefer proactive inline errors preventing over-dispatch rather than silent failures or negative stock balances.
3. **Auditability:** Operations leads require that every stock mutation records actor identity, timestamp, and reference number for accountability.
4. **Theme Preference:** High-contrast dark mode is favored in dimly lit warehouse environments to reduce eye strain.
