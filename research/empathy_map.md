# Empathy Map: Warehouse Operator

## Says
- "I need to know immediately if a critical part is low on stock before the shift starts."
- "Recording intake shouldn't take more than 5 seconds per batch."
- "I need clear visual indicators rather than dense text tables."

## Thinks
- "Will entering adjustments mess up the accounting records?"
- "Is the supplier delivery on schedule?"
- "I hope I don't accidentally dispatch more units than are physically on the shelf."

## Does
- Scans inventory daily across bins.
- Enters inbound quantities and checks packing slips.
- Alerts procurement when stock levels reach reorder points.

## Feels
- Frustrated by slow, clunky enterprise interfaces.
- Relieved when search yields instant SKU lookups.
- Confident when status badges clearly indicate inventory health.
